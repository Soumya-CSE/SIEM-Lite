SIEM LITE v1.0

A lightweight log analyzer that turns raw authentication logs into a readable security picture — failed logins, suspicious IPs, brute-force attempts and a composite threat score, in seconds.

What it does
Login analysis
Tallies successful and failed logins per user and flags accounts under repeated attack.
Suspicious IP detection
Groups failed attempts by source IP and scores each one Low, Medium or High risk.
Critical & brute-force detection
Surfaces explicit CRITICAL log lines plus inferred brute-force patterns as alerts.
Threat scoring & timeline
Combines every signal into a single 0–100 score and an hourly event timeline.
Persistent history
Every scan is saved so you can revisit past reports from the History tab.
